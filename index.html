Purpose-Built Architecture

Four Pillars of Grounded Care

Built without social vanity metrics, intrusive notifications, or clinical coldness. Every touchpoint is crafted to reduce cognitive load.

📊

1. Daily Mood Check-Ins

An intuitive, friction-free interface allowing teens to record their emotional state in seconds. Track frequencies across weeks to recognize triggers and early patterns of fatigue or sadness before they escalate.

✓ Micro-checkins in < 10 seconds
✍️

2. Private Somatic Journal

An unconstrained, secure space to articulate honest thoughts without fear of social judgment. Studies show expressive writing reduces amygdala reactivity and accelerates emotional processing during stressful academic seasons.

✓ 100% encrypted on your device
🌬️

3. Grounding & Nervous System Reset

Interactive physiological sigh and box-breathing routines designed to activate the parasympathetic nervous system during acute exam stress, panic, or social overwhelm.

✓ Instant somatic calm
💚

4. Immediate Crisis Pathways

Direct 1-tap connections to certified support infrastructure: the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, the Crisis Text Line (741741), and the National Alliance on Mental Illness (NAMI).

✓ 24/7 free, confidential emergency links

First-Hand Experience

"Why I Built HAAL: Turning Pain into a Sanctuary."

I built HAAL because I witnessed firsthand how mental health struggles can quietly devastate families and friends when there is no safe, private outlet.

“My grandmother has bipolar disorder, and my family saw her mental health deteriorate without social stimulation or an emotional outlet during the pandemic, and we had very limited ways to help her thousands of miles away. My grandma found solace in writing, which was a method of expressing one’s emotions that inspired me to create HAAL.

In middle school, I would see my classmates crying alone in the bathroom, and I have had friends tell me they were thinking of harming themselves. Oftentimes, teens feel like they have to suffer alone, or they simply don’t have the resources to reach out for help.

I hope that HAAL provides an outlet for high schoolers both in the United States and across the globe; with its ability to track the severity of one’s emotional state and recommend mental health services, hopefully, teens can seek help before it’s too late.”

HAAL was built on a simple conviction: emotional health tools for teenagers must never be monetized through advertising or personal data extraction. It must remain a safe, quiet, dignified harbor.

Clinical & Public Health Reality

The Youth Mental Health Emergency

According to federal health agencies, adolescent mental health crises have reached unprecedented heights. Accessible, confidential tools are an urgent necessity.

22%
High School Students at Risk
More than 1 in 5 high school students in the United States reported seriously considering suicide in the past year, an alarming rise from 16% a decade earlier.
Source: CDC Adolescent Health Report
#2
Leading Cause of Adolescent Death
Among children and teenagers between the ages of 15 and 19, suicide is the second leading cause of mortality in the United States.
Source: Boston Children’s Hospital Research
50%
Adolescent Lifetime Prevalence
Almost half of all adolescents experience a diagnosable mental health condition before reaching adulthood, yet fewer than 15% receive timely professional care.
Source: National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH)
