About HAAL

Created by Maya Sharma · Winner, 2023 Congressional App Challenge (IL-14)
The Mission: HAAL was created to provide young people with a completely private, judgment-free emotional sanctuary — pairing daily mood reflection with immediate, accessible crisis support.

The Origin Story

HAAL was conceived and developed by Maya Sharma, a student at Neuqua Valley High School in Naperville, Illinois. Watching peers navigate the intense pressures of high school academics, social changes, and emotional turbulence, Maya realized that while teens were increasingly open about mental health struggles, they lacked accessible, privacy-first tools designed specifically for their needs.

In 2023, Maya submitted HAAL to the Congressional App Challenge representing Illinois's 14th Congressional District, hosted by U.S. Representative Lauren Underwood. HAAL was selected as the Official District Winner for its intuitive interface, thoughtful feature set, and deep commitment to youth safety.

Why "HAAL"?

The word "Haal" (हाल) translates to state of being, emotional condition, or simply "how you are doing" in Hindi and Urdu. When friends ask "Kya haal hai?" ("How are you?"), it is an invitation for genuine, open connection. HAAL creates that quiet conversational check-in for the user, on their own device, every single day.

Core Design Pillars

  • 100% On-Device Privacy: No accounts required, no tracking, and no cloud data storage. Mood logs and journal entries never leave your personal phone.
  • Cognitive Behavioral Grounding: Mood tracking paired with emotional journaling encourages teens to identify triggers, notice patterns, and develop emotional literacy.
  • Immediate Crisis Safeguards: Embedded, one-tap access to national crisis lifelines including the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline and the Crisis Text Line (741741).
  • Zero Gamification or Advertising: No intrusive ads, streak shaming, or dopamine hooks. HAAL is built purely as a safe personal utility.

Recognition & Congressional Honor

As a Congressional App Challenge winner, HAAL was showcased to Members of Congress in Washington, D.C., during the annual #HouseOfCode event at the U.S. Capitol, standing among the most innovative student-developed software in the United States.
